# 32X support (experimental)

On a Sega CD with a 32X attached, the select screen shows MK CD's own portraits for the seven MK
fighters, drawn by the 32X, on every visit. The Genesis draws them in 14 colours; the 32X layer has
159. Without a 32X, the same disc plays exactly as the plain one. It is built on request
(`tools/build.py --32x`); the standard disc leaves it out until it does more (it was in the standard
disc from 2026-11-22 to 2026-11-23).

**Status: experimental.** It has been tested in emulators only (PicoDrive, ares, Genesis Plus GX),
never on hardware. It was developed as a separate experiment (2026-11-18 to 22) and came into the
build on 2026-11-22; its steps are in `tools/x32/` ([tools.md](tools.md)).

## Build and play

```
python3 tools/build.py --32x      # -> build/Eternal Kombat/Eternal Kombat.cue, with the 32X support
python3 tools/build.py            # the standard disc, without it
python3 tools/build.py --32x --out "build/Eternal Kombat 32X"   # beside the standard one (the gate's)
```

`tools/play.py` uses Genesis Plus GX, which has no 32X: the disc plays there as the plain one. To see
the 32X portraits, play the cue in an emulator that runs 32X CD games:
- PicoDrive (the RetroArch core, and what `tools/lib/pico.py` drives for the checks);
- ares.

## How it works

`tools/build.py --32x` builds the plain disc, then `tools/lib/build32x.py` (`add_32x`) runs `tools/x32/`'s
five steps on it, then writes the CD cues' disc times again (`patches.cd_times`): step 3 grows
`GAME.CO`, which moves the audio tracks 40 sectors on (until 2026-11-23 every cue - FINISH HIM, the
fatality music, the Pit's - started half a second late on this disc). The steps:

1. **Startup** (`32x_art_probe.py`, as reduced in step 4): MAIN.BIN's startup calls a routine at
   `$51A0`. If the 32X answers (`MARS` at `$A130EC`), it requests the adapter.
2. **The portraits** (`32x_cd_roster_preview.py`): the seven MK portraits from `extracted/`
   (prepare.py decodes them from your MK disc), packed for the 32X framebuffer with one shared
   palette.
3. **The payload** (`32x_select_payload_probe.py`):
   - appended to `GAME.CO`: the portraits, EC's cursor frames and palette (read from your EC disc),
     the 32X renderer and the cursor hook;
   - the sub CPU loads it with `GAME.CO`, into PRG RAM up to `$28000`;
   - MAIN.BIN's VBlank call (`$53A`, `jsr $FF1540`) goes to a startup routine at `$FF5940`
     instead. Once, it boots the 32X's SH-2s, copies the payload to the 32X, and installs the
     renderer (`$FF63FA`) and the cursor hook (`$FF6900`) in main-CPU scratch RAM.
   - The hook redraws EC's cursors over the 32X portraits, and hides the 32X layer while a
     confirmation animation plays.
4. **Repairs** (`32x_repair_experiment.py`):
   - the startup is reduced to detection and the adapter request (an old test drew a portrait
     over the warning screen);
   - when select exits, the VBlank call goes back to EC's `$FF1540`. The fight reused the hook's
     scratch RAM and hung on a black screen.
   - the 32X layer is enabled once the select screen is ready.
5. **Every visit** (`32x_reentry.py`): the exit hands VBlank to the coordinator instead of EC's
   routine, and clears the transfer's state.
   - The coordinator calls EC's routine first. Through a fight it only reads main RAM and the 32X's
     registers.
   - When it sees the select screen's signature again, it calls a small arm routine (MAIN.BIN
     `$5BBA`). That routine clears the renderer's state, so the payload is copied and the
     renderer and hook installed again, as on the first visit.
   - The renderer's state RAM is the game's between visits. A first try cleared it at the exit:
     by the next select it held game data, and the game froze.

Same inputs, same disc: `tools/dev/verify_fresh.py` builds the disc in a fresh clone and compares it.

## Checks

Every check in allchecks plays the disc without a 32X (Genesis Plus GX). `tools/checks/x32check.py`
also plays the same controller route with a 32X (PicoDrive) and without one:
- the warning screen is clean;
- the first select screen shows the 32X portraits with a 32X and the Genesis ones without;
- a Duel fight starts and player 1 responds;
- the match ends back on the select screen.

Two matches: three visits to select, each with its portraits. It fails on the experiment's earlier disc
(the stray portrait, the hang before the fight), on the disc before step 5 (Genesis portraits on the second
visit), and caught the first re-entry try freezing.

## Open

1. **Hardware:** none of the following is proven on a real Sega CD + 32X (nor the detection on a
   Sega CD without one: it reads the 32X's ID at `$A130EC`, where nothing answers):
   - the SH-2 startup;
   - bus arbitration;
   - VBlank timing;
   - the scratch RAM (`$FF63FA`, `$FF6900`, `$FF7600`, under `$28000` in PRG RAM).
2. **The Tournament** isn't checked. The check plays Duel. Two Contest matches were played by hand
   in PicoDrive (2026-11-22: the 32X portraits at select, both fights and the statistics between
   them, no hang); Contest doesn't pass through select between matches.
3. **Confirmations:** the 32X layer is hidden while EC's confirmation burst plays, so the
   Genesis portraits show for that moment.
